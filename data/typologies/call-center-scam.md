# Call-centre impersonation scam (แก๊งคอลเซ็นเตอร์)

Pattern: victims are phoned by people posing as police, a bank or a courier and told to move money
to a "safe" account for verification. Victims usually make one to three large transfers to accounts
they have never paid before, often during working hours.

Indicators on the receiving side: inflows from many unrelated people who have no prior relationship
with the account; transfer sizes far above the account's normal activity; immediate onward transfers.
Indicators on the victim side: first-ever transfer to that counterparty, unusually large, possibly
from a device or location the customer does not normally use.
