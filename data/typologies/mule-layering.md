# Mule account layering (บัญชีม้าหลายทอด)

Pattern: stolen or scammed funds land in a first-layer "collector" mule account, are split and
forwarded within minutes to second-layer accounts, then to cash-out accounts that buy gold,
phones or crypto, or withdraw cash. Each hop keeps a small cut.

Indicators: account opened recently; thin KYC; money out within minutes of money in (pass-through,
out/in ratio above 90%); many first-time senders; several accounts operated from the same device;
round-number splits; activity concentrated in a few days.

Typical response: trace funds from the victim forward, freeze accounts where money is still
sitting, file an STR covering the whole network, open recovery follow-ups for victims.
