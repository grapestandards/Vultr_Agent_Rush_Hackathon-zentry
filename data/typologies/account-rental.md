# Rented or sold accounts (เช่า/ขายบัญชี)

Pattern: people open wallets or bank accounts and hand the credentials to a syndicate for a fee.
The syndicate operates many such accounts from a small number of phones.

Indicators: several unrelated customers' accounts logging in from the same device; accounts opened
in a short burst; little genuine everyday spending (only a few small purchases to look normal);
account owner details that do not match the transaction pattern (e.g., student account moving
hundreds of thousands of baht).
